What to practice

Spaces and capitals do not matter. Option order does not matter for AN (/K C /A*S is the same as /A*S /K C). A leading zero on the day is fine (05JAN or 5JAN).

Line numbers. Use them with DO or DM (DO 3, DM 3).

What is the time at the far right of an availability line?
The elapsed time (total flight journey time), for example 3:25.

What is the third way to check availability for a flight, besides the line display?
Hover over the flight number.
From your notes
What do DY and EFT mean?
DY = day (of the week). EFT = elapsed flight time.
From your notes
Which time should you always read when comparing arrivals?
The arrival time, which is local time in the arrival country.
From your notes
In a city pair like MNLHKG, which is the origin?
MNL, the first three letters. HKG is the destination.
How is time written in a command?
HHMM in 24 hours, so 3:00 PM is 1500.
HH = hour, MM = minute
What is the difference between AN and TN?
AN shows availability (seats open to sell). TN shows the timetable (schedule only).
What does the 3-letter connection pair KUL-KUL stand for?
A connection at the same airport (arrive and depart KUL).
